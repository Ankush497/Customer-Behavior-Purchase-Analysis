
# 🛍️ Customer Behaviour & Purchase Analysis (Power BI Dashboard)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on Customer Behaviour and Purchase Analysis using Microsoft Power BI.

The objective is to transform customer purchase data into meaningful business insights by analysing customer demographics, product preferences, seasonal trends, subscription patterns, purchase frequency, and spending behaviour.

An interactive Power BI dashboard has been developed to help understand customer purchasing patterns, identify popular product categories, and explore opportunities for improving customer engagement and business performance.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is designed with interactive slicers, KPI cards, and multiple visualizations to explore customer purchasing behaviour.

### 🔹 1. Customer Category Analysis

- Compare customer purchases across product categories.
- Identify the most popular product categories.
- Understand differences in product demand.

### 🔹 2. Seasonal Purchase Analysis

- Analyse customer purchasing patterns across four seasons.
- Compare Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter.
- Identify seasonal demand variations.

### 🔹 3. Subscription Analysis

- Compare subscribed and non-subscribed customers.
- Understand subscription adoption.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

### 🔹 4. Age Group Purchase Analysis

- Compare previous and current purchases across age groups.
- Analyse purchasing behaviour among Young Adults, Middle-aged, Senior, and Adult customers.
- Understand differences in spending patterns.

### 🔹 5. Product Size Analysis

- Analyse purchase value across different product sizes.
- Compare M, L, S, and XL categories.
- Identify the size category generating the highest purchase value.

### 🔹 6. Purchase Frequency Analysis

- Analyse customer purchasing frequency.
- Compare Every 3 Months, Fortnightly, Annually, Monthly, and Weekly segments.
- Understand purchase count and purchase value trends.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following insights were identified:

1. **Customer Base:** The dataset represents approximately 3.90K customers with a total purchase value of $233K.

2. **Category Performance:** Clothing leads with 1,737 purchases, followed by Accessories with 1,240.

3. **Seasonal Trends:** Spring records the highest seasonal customer count at 999, followed by Fall (975), Winter (971), and Summer (955).

4. **Subscription Behaviour:** 73% of customers are non-subscribers, while 27% have an active subscription.

5. **Age Group Analysis:** Young Adults record the highest current purchase value at approximately $62K.

6. **Size Preferences:** Medium (M) generates the highest purchase value at $105K, followed by Large (L) at $62K.

7. **Purchase Frequency:** Every 3 Months has the highest purchase count at 1,147, followed by Fortnightly at 1,089.
